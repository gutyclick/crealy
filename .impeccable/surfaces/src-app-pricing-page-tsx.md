Intent: Persuade creators and small teams to choose capacity rather than feature gates across four truthful tiers.
Primary action: Start Free or open secure Stripe Checkout for Starter, Creator, or Pro in monthly or annual billing.
Hierarchy: “¿Cuánto quieres crear?” thesis; accessible period control; four result-led cards; Creator is dominant; credit-combination strip; concise FAQ.
Visual world: Crealy production table — matte warm blacks, editorial spacing, lime #DDF527 only for readiness/action, Geist hierarchy, no decorative glass or gradients.
Truth constraints: Public prices are Free $0, Starter $5, Creator $15, Pro $39; annual totals $48/$144/$374. Checkout only opens when the exact plan-period Price ID exists. Credits have no fixed monetary value.
States: Logged out CTA routes to signup/login; logged in checkout shows loading/error; disabled billing communicates unavailability; checkout cancellation is visible but calm.
Motion: One short clipped plan reveal and restrained hover lift; content remains visible and reduced-motion safe.
Responsive: 320px minimum, single column on mobile, no horizontal scrolling, controls at least 44px.
